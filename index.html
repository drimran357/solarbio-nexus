About BioSolar Nexus

Integrated infrastructure for resilient communities

Technology

One modular platform. Multiple energy and resource flows.

BioSolar Nexus combines mature technologies in a new integrated configuration, with thermal management and digital control designed to maintain robust operation in demanding conditions.

Rather than treating energy, sanitation and agricultural resources as separate challenges, the platform connects them: organic feedstocks support biogas production; biogas and solar resources supply electricity and heat; thermal storage balances demand; and digestate provides a route for nutrient recovery.
