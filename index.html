Waste to resilience.

BioSolar Nexus is developing and demonstrating a modular renewable energy and resource recovery system for crisis affected communities in Ukraine. The project combines anaerobic digestion, solar photovoltaic thermal panels, micro combined heat and power, phase change material thermal storage, battery storage and digital monitoring. The aim is to convert locally available organic waste and solar energy into reliable electricity, useful heat, hot water, renewable biogas and nutrient rich digestate.

About the project

Integrated infrastructure for resilient communities

BioSolar Nexus brings renewable generation, organic waste valorisation, thermal management and digital control together in one integrated system. The project is designed for decentralised operation in cold climate conditions and responds directly to the need for more resilient local energy infrastructure. The system is intended to demonstrate how farms and communities can obtain useful energy from resources that are already available locally, while reducing waste and recovering nutrients for agricultural use.

Technology

One platform. Multiple energy and resource flows.

The system integrates mature technologies in a new configuration so energy, waste and nutrient flows can be managed together rather than as separate challenges.

Organic feedstocks support biogas production; solar PVT and CHP provide electricity and useful heat; PCM thermal storage supports temperature management; batteries improve electrical autonomy; and IoT-enabled monitoring supports diagnostics and optimisation.

Project progress

From design to demonstration

The project has progressed through system architecture, detailed design, control strategy development, site assessment and major procurement activities. Fabrication and local integration are now preparing the system for mechanical and electrical assembly, bench testing, commissioning and the subsequent demonstration phase. Public progress information is presented at a level that communicates delivery without disclosing sensitive infrastructure or precise site details.
